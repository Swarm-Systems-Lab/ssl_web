The Swarm Systems Lab explores fixed-wing aircraft, rotorcraft, rovers, and other autonomous robots. We are interested in how local interactions can produce useful, robust behaviour across a group of machines.

The lab shares research, experiments, and opportunities here. For conversations with researchers and people beginning to explore swarm robotics, join our Discord community.
