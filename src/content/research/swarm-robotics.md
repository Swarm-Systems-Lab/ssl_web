title: Swarm robotics and autonomous systems
role: Research area
summary: We study collective behaviour in groups of autonomous robots.

Our work sits at the intersection of control theory, autonomous systems, and emergent behaviour. We use experiments and analysis to understand how simple local rules can coordinate many agents.

The lab works across aerial and ground platforms, with research questions shaped by the capabilities and constraints of real robots.
