Our only submission to ICRA has been accepted! Our work titled Distributed coordinated path following using guiding vector fields shows how to guarantee formally the global convergence of a team of agents to a desired path in arbitrary dimensions and with a desired inter-agent configuration. Experiments with two fixed-wings and simulations with hundreds of agents show appealing applications for formation flights and volume coverage.
