title: "Today, we demonstrated our drone technology to a delegation from the Spanish Army, including two generals"
date: 2026-06-18
---
Today, we demonstrated our drone technology to a delegation from the Spanish Army, including two generals. Although our research is not military in nature, we recognize the growing importance of drone technology for national security. During the visit, we shared our expertise with MADOC, the branch responsible for integrating innovation into military doctrine and procedures, discussing the next generation of autonomous drone systems.
