The swarm is the infrastructure. That is the idea behind [Aerosense](/research/aerosense), our ERC Proof of Concept at the Swarm Systems Lab.

A new drone is not one more thing to locate; it is one more reference for the others. More drones mean more links, more redundancy and more robustness. That is what lets each drone stay simple and cheap: it only requires a microcontroller, and it is 100% deterministic.

The swarm builds its own localization and communication network at up to 100 Hz. That is fast enough for agile robots such as racing drones or our acrobatic planes.

Decentralized and scalable by design, with all computations onboard: deploy it anywhere, anytime.

In today's flight, four racing drones fly within our line of sight for visual validation.
