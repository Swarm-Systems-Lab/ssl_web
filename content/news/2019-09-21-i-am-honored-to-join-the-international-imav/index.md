I am honored to join the International IMAV Comittee! IMAV is a competition and conference event on Mini and Micro Aerial Vehicles that was born in Europe in 2009. After several consecutive years held in Europe, IMAV aimed to be spread worldwide starting in Beijing 2016. IMAV currently gather around 200 researcher all over the world to demonstrate their capabilities for fulfilling new challenges in drone autonomous missions.
