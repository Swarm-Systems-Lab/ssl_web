Our paper titled Forced variational integrator for distance-based shape control with flocking behavior of multi-agent systems has been accepted at IFAC World Congress 2020, Berlin! This was a joint work with the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (ICMAT) in Spain, and the University of Groningen in the Netherlands.
