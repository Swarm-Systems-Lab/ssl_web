---
title: "Héctor starts as a research fellow at the Universidad Complutense of Madrid under the program Atracción de Talento cofunded by the goverment of the Autonomous Community of Madrid"
date: 2020-04-01
---
Héctor starts as a research fellow at the Universidad Complutense of Madrid under the program Atracción de Talento cofunded by the goverment of the Autonomous Community of Madrid. He will continue his research in multi-agent systems and robotics in the Deparment of Computer Architecture and Automatic Control at the Faculty of Physics in Madrid.
