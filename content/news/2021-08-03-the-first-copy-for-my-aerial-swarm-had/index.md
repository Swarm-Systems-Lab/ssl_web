---
title: "The first copy for Héctor's aerial swarm had a successful maiden flight!"
date: 2021-08-03
---
The first copy for Héctor's aerial swarm had a successful maiden flight! In fact, this is his first autonomous flight at his new club in Arroyomolinos. Nice to be licensed to fly aeromodels again.

The aircraft is a Sonicmodell AR.Wing 900 geared with Paparazzi, the open-source autopilot.
