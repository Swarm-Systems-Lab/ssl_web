Paper accepted in Applied Mathematics and Computation!

Our paper titled Directional Mollification for Knot-Preserving C∞ Smoothing of Polygonal Chains with Explicit Curvature Bounds, by Alfredo González-Calvin, Juan F. Jiménez and Hector, has been accepted in Applied Mathematics and Computation.

We introduce a directional mollification operator that turns a polygonal chain into a C∞ curve that stays arbitrarily close to the original one while passing exactly through its vertices. The smoothing is local, so changing one segment only affects the curve in a controllable neighborhood, and the curvature comes with closed-form bounds. This is useful for generating smooth paths for robots, computer graphics and CNC machining.
