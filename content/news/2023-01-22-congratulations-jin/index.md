Congratulations Jin! We had an accepted paper in the American Control Conference on..... of course, formation control:P. The title of the article is Distance-based Formation-Motion Control for Unicycle Agents written by Jin Chen (Ph.D. student in Groningen), Bayu Jawardhana, and me. In this article Jin provides answers to some open questions, such as the exponential converge, of a team of unicycles following distance-based formation controllers with velocity tracking.

5/12/2022 I gave a talk on swarm robotics and formation control at the Beijing University of Technology. Hopefully, next time it will be in person:P.
