Our work on how to coordinate unicycles without orientation measurements has been accepted for publication! We prove rigorously intuitive results; as we can see in the figure, the vehicles' alignments are under control if you control distance-rigid formations of moving unicycles using only relative positions.

J. Chen, B. Jayawardhana, HG de Marina. Distributed Distance-based Formation-Motion Control of Unicycle Agents without Orientation Measurements. To appear in Transactions on Control of Network Systems. 2024.
