title: "We were contacted by the ICRA's organization today"
date: 2021-04-23
---
We were contacted by the ICRA's organization today. Our submission Distributed coordinated path following using guiding vector fields has been nominated for the BEST PAPER AWARD together with other three finalists! There were 4005 submission, and only four were selected among 1946 accepted works. The authors of this paper are Weijia Yao (University of Groningen), Héctor García de Marina (University Complutense of Madrid), Zhiyong Sun (TU Eindhoven), and Ming Cao (University of Groningen). Congrats!
