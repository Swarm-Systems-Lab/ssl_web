title: "Héctor has been awarded the Ramón y Cajal grant!"
date: 2021-08-05
---
Héctor has been awarded the Ramón y Cajal grant! That is (arguably) the most prestigious Spanish grant for young investigators, and it guarantees five years of independent research funded by the Spanish government. It is an excellent opportunity to think and organize the following steps on his research on swarm robotics.
