Martin Zoula is visiting the Swarm Systems Lab for the whole month of October. He comes from the Multi-robot Systems (MRS) group at the Czech Technical University in Prague, and his PhD focuses on drone communications.

During his stay we have been running experiments with our Aerosense system, the ultra-wideband avionics network for drone swarms that we are developing in our ERC Proof of Concept project [Aerosense](/research/aerosense). Thanks for the visit, Martin, it has been great having you here!
