Our paper titled Maneuvering formations of mobile agents using designed mismatched angles has been accepted in IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control. In this work, Liangming Chen from the University of Groningen explores and analyzes how formations can be maneuvered by adding mismatches to the interior angles to be controlled.
