In this demonstration, we show how two fixed-wing drones can rendezvous, fly in formation, split, and meet again at a different location - all while flying at 18 m/s. The key is not about controlling the speed; the drones adjust their trajectories cooperatively to ensure successful rendezvous - all guidance runs entirely onboard. With this strategy, wind is no longer a major obstacle.
