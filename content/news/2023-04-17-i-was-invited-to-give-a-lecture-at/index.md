Héctor was invited to give a lecture at the 2023 IEEE RAS Summer School on Multi-Robot Systems together with other researchers like Dario Floreano and Javier Alonso-Mora, organized by the Czech Technical University (CTU) in Prague.

He will present his latest results on source-seeking with resilient robot swarms. Hope to see you there!
