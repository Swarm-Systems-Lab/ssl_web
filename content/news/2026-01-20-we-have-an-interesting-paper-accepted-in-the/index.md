We have an interesting paper accepted in the American Control Conference titled Linear quadratic control for discrete-time systems with stochastic and bounded noises led by the postdoc Xuehui Ma.

In this work, we show for the first time how to handle stochastic noise that is also bounded simultaneously for estimation filters.
