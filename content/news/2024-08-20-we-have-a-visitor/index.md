We have a visitor! Yu Zhuo from INRIA, Lille, France. He is a Ph.D. student under the supervision of Andrey Polyakov and Gang Zheng, and his research is on Guiding Vector Fields for autonomous vehicles. During his visit, we will integrate some of this work into our UAVs.

In the photo, we were flying over a nearby lake taking scientific data over a calibrated weather station so a team of physicists can estimate the local wind field.
