date: 2024-12-06

Professor Heiko Hamann from the University of Konstanz, who is specialized in robot swarms, is visiting us for two weeks! He gave an inspiring talk on swarm robotics; it was very interesting to know about how adding more robots to the network can decrease the performance of the team, and how hysteresis plays a role in order to remove robots and recover the original performance of the swarm.

Of course, we took him to the field and show him some demos with our aircraft:P.
