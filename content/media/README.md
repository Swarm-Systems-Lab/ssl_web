Drop photos, GIFs, and short clips in this folder and they appear on the
media page automatically. Name them

    YYYY-MM-DD_short-caption.jpg

for example  2026-04-17_group-photo-in-granada.jpg  which shows as
"Group photo in granada" dated 2026-04-17.

Accepted: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif .gif .mp4 .webm
Photos are resized automatically. GIFs and clips are served as they are, so
keep them small — under a few megabytes.

Long videos belong on YouTube; list them in content/media.yaml instead.

Worked examples for every kind of picture: ../README.md#photos-gifs-and-video
