---
title: "Distance mismatches and collaborative transport"
summary: "Injecting distance disagreements to create 3D motions in rigid formations, and using the resulting stability properties to carry a heavy load with a team of rotorcraft."
order: 6
tags: ["control", "rotorcraft"]
---

We inject distance disagreements to create 3D motions in rigid formations. We
exploit the stability and convergence properties from the theory to reject the
tension forces coming from a heavy load carried by the team.

## Selected papers

- HG de Marina, B. Jayawardhana, M. Cao. *Taming mismatches in inter-agent distances for the formation-motion control of second-order agents*. IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2018.
- HG de Marina, E. Smeur. *Flexible collaborative transportation by a team of rotorcraft*. IEEE ICRA 2019.
