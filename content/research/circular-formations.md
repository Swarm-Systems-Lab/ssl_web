The Swarm Systems Lab has developed a fully distributed algorithm that resembles
the tornado schooling of fish.

The combination of a smooth guiding vector field, an adaptive control barrier
function, and a scalable overtaking rule on the outside provides formal
guarantees to avoid collisions while a swarm of unicycle robots orbits a convex
path at constant but different speeds.
