We show global convergence for the motion coordination of swarms on embedded
surfaces in 3D. In particular, we exploit guiding vector fields that are free of
singularities thanks to our topological surgery.

Guiding vector fields have grown into one of the lab's main lines of work: we
organised the first workshop dedicated to them at IROS 2025.
