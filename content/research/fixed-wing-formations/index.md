---
title: "Fixed-wing formations at constant speed"
summary: "Controlling formations of fixed-wing UAVs that fly at different constant speeds, with theoretical guarantees and flight validation."
order: 5
tags: ["uav", "field"]
---

We control formations of fixed-wing UAVs flying at different constant speeds,
and we provide both theoretical guarantees and their experimental validation in
the field. We also exploit the convergence properties of a guidance vector field
together with consensus algorithms to control the rendezvous of a team of
aircraft flying at constant speed.
