title: "Resilient source seeking"
summary: "Robot swarms that locate a source through emergent behaviour, staying resilient to malfunctioning robots and to noise in actuators and sensors."

---

We address the source-seeking problem with a robot swarm. The distributed
emergent behaviour helps the robots navigate, remaining resilient even with
malfunctioning robots and with noise in actuators and sensors. We rigorously
prove the effectiveness of the algorithm even for teams of unicycles moving at
constant speed, such as fixed-wing aircraft.

What matters for resilient source seeking is how dispersed the formation is,
rather than any particular symmetry. The result is fully distributed, so agents
can leave and join the group.
