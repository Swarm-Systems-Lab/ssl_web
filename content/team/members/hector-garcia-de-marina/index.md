He runs the Swarm Systems Lab. He is a Ramón y Cajal researcher in Computer
Engineering, Automation and Robotics (ICAR) and at the Institute of Mathematics
of Granada (IMAG), University of Granada.

He is a recipient of the ERC Starting Grant, associate editor of the *IEEE
Transactions on Robotics*, and a proud developer of the open-source drone
project [Paparazzi](https://github.com/paparazzi/paparazzi).
